machines ==grow into independence==: robots that need less human guidance, and
students who become engineers and researchers in their own right.

<div class="ev-about__text" markdown>

My work follows three connected tracks. Research gives my teaching real
problems to work on. Teaching brings students close to that research. Mentoring
turns the strongest of them into independent engineers, and their theses often
become the next papers. AI is the thread through all three: what I study, what
I teach, and how I build programs for others to learn it.

I lecture at the Metropolitan University of Tirana, where I am Program Director
for AI and am completing my PhD on autonomous navigation. At Holberton School I
lead AI education and the machine learning curriculum across its international
campuses. Away from work you will find me at a chessboard or with a book of
poems.

</div>
