# Mentoring

A good mentor makes themselves less necessary over time. My aim is for every
student to leave able to ==plan their own next move==.
{ .ev-lead }

</div>

## How a thesis works with me

<ol class="ev-waypoints">
<li><span class="ev-waypoints__when">Step 1</span><strong>Pick a topic</strong><p>Choose one from the open topics or bring your own idea. We meet once to check it fits the time you have.</p></li>
<li><span class="ev-waypoints__when">Step 2</span><strong>Write a one-page plan</strong><p>The question, the approach, and what "done" looks like. This page is our agreement.</p></li>
<li><span class="ev-waypoints__when">Step 3</span><strong>Build and test</strong><p>Short meetings every two weeks. You bring progress and problems; we decide the next step together.</p></li>
<li><span class="ev-waypoints__when">Step 4</span><strong>Write it up</strong><p>You write, I review chapter by chapter. Strong theses can become a paper.</p></li>
<li class="ev-waypoints__now"><span class="ev-waypoints__when">Step 5</span><strong>Defend</strong><p>We rehearse the presentation at least once before the real one.</p></li>
</ol>

## What I expect, and what you can expect

<div class="ev-two" markdown>
<div class="ev-two__col" markdown>

**From you**

- Steady work, not a sprint at the end
- Honest updates, including when you are stuck
- Code and writing you can explain line by line

</div>
<div class="ev-two__col" markdown>

**From me**

- Regular, focused meetings
- Clear feedback within a week
- Help turning good work into a publication

</div>
